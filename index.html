    Questions

    Before you call us

    Is the first consultation really free?

    Yes. We meet, understand your plot or space, and explain how we'd approach it. There's no obligation to continue.

    I've just bought a plot. Where do I start?

    Book a free consultation and bring your plot papers or layout plan. We'll discuss what you can build and suggest next steps before you spend on anything.

    Can you design both the house and the interiors?

    Yes. Doing both together usually saves time and avoids changes on site later.

    Will you visit the site during construction?

    Yes, when you choose design with site supervision. We check work against the drawings and resolve issues with your contractor on the spot.

    Do you help with sanction drawings?

    Yes. We prepare the drawings needed for sanction and coordinate with the required consultants.

    Do you work outside Nagpur?

    Yes, across the Nagpur region, including a shop in Lakhni and a bungalow on the National Highway.
